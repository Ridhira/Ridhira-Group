Srinath Nalluri

Senior VP

Srinath Nalluri is a Mechanical Engineer and an MBA from IIM Bangalore. He has worked in corporates, for more than 30 years, across India, in companies like Kotak Bank, Sodexo, Genpact and Vodafone India. He has worked across functions like Sales, Marketing and Operations including a stint as a Master Black Belt in Genpact. He has been a member of the management team at Ode Spa for more than eight years.
