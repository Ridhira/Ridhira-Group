Our Story

Built circa 1863
Portuguese Goan history, restored.

Casa De Mello is one of Saligao’s enduring Portuguese homes, first shaped by the De Mello family, whose imprint remains in its arches, courtyards, and timeless details. The home carries the elegance of an era when Goan life blended European influence with local tradition.

Sunlit courtyards, carved windows, and cool tiled floors reveal a lineage of craftsmanship and memory. Restored with care as a Ridhira Retreat, this historic home invites guests to slow down. It is a place where wellness unfolds within a setting where heritage and emotion live side by side.

The Best of Goa,
Close By.

Set in the serene town of Saligao, Casa De Mello places you close to the best of North Goa while keeping you away from its rush. Candolim’s beaches, Calangute’s energy and Baga’s nightlife are just a short drive away.

For those drawn to heritage and architecture, Goa’s churches and historic quarters offer stories at every turn. Cafés, local markets, art galleries, and cultural trails are all within easy reach.
