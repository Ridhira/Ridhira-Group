Our Philosophy

Membership is not a transaction.
It is a way of belonging.

Tiger Hills stands apart from what Ridhira Retreats is known for. It is the crown of Udaipur’s social space, where access is limited to those who have experienced the world yet continue to seek something beyond it. It is a place where members immerse themselves in conversations that begin at dusk and may not end until the hills catch the first light of morning.

"The finest experiences are not bought. They are granted. They come with trust, and with people who feel like your own."

We draw our soul from Rajputana hospitality, expressed through a contemporary sensibility that is globally aware and locally rooted. The result is something rarely seen in the City of Lakes, a club that feels grounded in its past and firmly in the present.

The Cipher

A cipher is both a code and a key. It unlocks what remains unseen, and marks entry into the inner circle. Members carry that key shaped by the world’s most selective private clubs, The Cipher takes form in Udaipur. It reflects a global standard, expressed within a distinct setting.

The World Within

Four layers.
One world.
A glimpse.

Each space carries its own atmosphere, its own code of entry, and its own rhythm. Access deepens as your membership does from the rooftop sky bar to the most private room in Udaipur.

01
Altaire - The Sky Bar

Perched above the city, the night moves to a rhythm shaped by Indian and international artists, crafted cocktails, and a crowd that knows where to be.

For Cipher members, the evening starts before the city arrives.

02
Veya - The Café

Samosa or baklava. The choice is yours. It always is. Here, conversations begin easily, and often return.

As you savour the finest single-origin Ethiopian coffee or the first flush Darjeeling tea.

Here, conversations remain within, and the experience gathers around the table. The menu evolves, the bar is thoughtfully curated, and each visit reflects a level of detail reserved for those within.
