Vijay Pandey

CEO

Vijay Pandey is the Chief Executive Officer of Ridhira Living, where he spearheads the development of the world's largest chain of wellness-themed resort living communities. He holds a degree from the Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur. With over 30 years of experience, Vijay has held prominent leadership roles, including CEO at AEPL and Essel Group, and was President at Puravankara Projects Limited. His extensive expertise and strategic vision are pivotal in driving Ridhira Living's success.
